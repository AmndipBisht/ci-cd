Our story

Northline started as a two-person team experimenting with small, well-made things. Over time that turned into a habit of shipping projects that are simple to explain and easy to maintain — which is also why this dummy site is deliberately plain.

How we work

Every project moves through the same three stages: understand the problem, build the smallest version that solves it, then refine based on what we learn. No stage gets skipped, and no stage gets more time than it needs.
